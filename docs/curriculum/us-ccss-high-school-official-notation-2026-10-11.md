# US CCSS high-school source notation corrections — three source-verified examples

Three accepted *immutable* source standards flattened meaningful superscripts and rational exponents. The independently inspected official standards source confirms correct notation:
- `CCSS:HSA-SSE.A.2`: `x⁴ − y⁴` as `(x²)² − (y²)²` and factorization `(x² − y²)(x² + y²)`. Source: https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/HSA/SSE/
- `CCSS:HSA-APR.C.4`: `(x² + y²)² = (x² − y²)² + (2xy)²`. Source: https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/HSA/APR/
- `CCSS:HSN-RN.A.1`: `5^(1/3)` as cube root; `(5^(1/3))³ = 5^((1/3)3)`. Source: https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/HSN/RN/
