# Rich Lesson Content V2 — Polish Independent Authoring Research Set

**Programme:** R8 catalogue-wide rollout  
**Scope:** `PL-NATIONAL-MATH` learner-facing lessons  
**Purpose:** Resolve the pedagogical-research gap without changing Polish official curriculum identity or copying curriculum/legal prose into the lesson body.

## Authoring rule

The Polish National Curriculum / ZPE / ELI remains the authority for **what** each lesson targets.

Edulytics authors the learner-facing explanation independently.

The references below are used for mathematical/pedagogical cross-checking and method coverage. They are **not** copied wholesale into Edulytics, and they do not replace the Polish curriculum target.

## Fail-closed source rules

1. A reference is used only for topics it actually covers.
2. A lesson does not inherit another curriculum's objective or wording.
3. No CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA, CC BY-ND, All Rights Reserved or unknown-license source becomes an adaptation source.
4. Edulytics-generated worked examples come from the existing `READY_VERIFIED` Practice contract for the exact lesson and are solved/independently verified by the Mathematics engine.
5. Official Polish legal/curriculum wording remains provenance and target evidence; it is not copied as the instructional lesson body.
6. If a lesson cannot be localized and compiled safely, it remains on the existing canonical Polish body rather than receiving English fallback prose.

## Attribution policy

Where Edulytics directly adapts CC BY material in a future reviewed static sidecar, the final published material must carry the required attribution, licence link and change indication.

The R8 runtime authoring layer is designed as **independent Edulytics authoring**. These sources are research/cross-check references; the runtime layer does not reproduce source prose or source illustrations.
