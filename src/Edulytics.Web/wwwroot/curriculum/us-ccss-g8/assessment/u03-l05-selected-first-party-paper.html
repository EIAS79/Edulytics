5.1 • Fraction division warm-up

Calculate 2 5/8 ÷ 1/2 as an exact fraction and decimal. Show why dividing by 1/2 doubles the first number.

Explain one common error involving mixed-number division and correct it.

5.2 • Cup stacks and starting height

Six identical stacked cups measure 15 cm high; twelve measure 23 cm. Find the constant additional height per cup and the model's initial height.

Predict modeled stack height at 18 cups and explain why the linear graph does NOT pass through the origin.

How many whole cups are required for a model height of at least 50 cm? Distinguish solving a real number equation from counting whole cups.

5.3 • Connect slope to rate of change

Plot the measured cup stacks on a coordinate grid and construct a slope triangle. Label x as number of cups and y as centimeters.

Compare y=(4/3)x+7 against y=(4/3)x and explain what the 7 cm represents.

Independent Practice • Nonproportional models

P1: Two pizzas delivered cost $25 including one delivery fee; three pizzas delivered cost $34. What is the price per pizza and delivery fee? Write total cost.

P2: A painter charges $500 for supplies plus $50 per labor hour. Calculate costs for 3 and 8 hours.

P3: A runner starts 500 m ahead and moves 4 m/s while another starts at 0 and runs 5 m/s. Write two linear equations and determine the meeting time.

P4: Find equation of line through (2,5),(6,7), including slope and intercept.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down • Initial value matters

A water tank starts with 12 liters and fills 3 liters every minute. Write the line equation and make a table at 0,2,5 minutes.

Explain why this is linear but not proportional and identify intercept and slope with units.
