6.1 • Growing tile patterns

For pattern number n, a yellow square array uses n² tiles; blue strips use 3n tiles, and a red strip uses n tiles. At n=4 and n=10 calculate each color and the total.

Decide whether the total n²+4n is linear. Explain why blue and red counts each change by a constant rate while the yellow count does not.

6.3 • Summer reading balance

A student has read 30 pages before a reading plan starts and reads 40 further pages every day. Make a four-day table for total completed pages and graph it.

Write p=30+40d. Explain which part represents starting progress and how the slope differs from the y-intercept.

Independent Practice • Read the intercept

P1: A phone has 40 minutes of credit and loses 2 minutes after each paid call. What do slope and intercept mean?

P2: A gym charges $30 to join, then $8 per class. Calculate total for 4 classes.

P3: A brownie recipe needs 2 cups flour per 1 cup sugar. Explain why the graph goes through origin if no ingredients are added initially.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down • Two graphs, two starting points

Compare A: y=10+3x and B: y=4+3x. Draw and explain their shared rate and different intercepts.

Which has the greater output at x=5, and by how much? Show two correct values and justify why the lines are parallel.
