7.1 ? Which are the Same? Which are Different?

Match an equation, a table, a graph description, and a story that all represent a $5 start fee plus $2 per item. Explain two features that prove the match.

7.2 ? Comparing Temperatures

Two temperature functions are shown as a table and a graph. Compare their values at the same time and identify when one is warmer.

7.3 ? Comparing Volumes

Use a table and equation to compare two containers filling at constant but different rates. Identify initial values and rates.

7.4 ? It?s Not a Race

Explain why two function graphs can be compared at equal inputs without treating the context as a race. State what an intersection means.
