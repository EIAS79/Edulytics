9.2 — Parallel Lines

Begin with two distinct horizontal lines ℓ: y=1 and m: y=4 (separation 3 units). Apply each movement independently to BOTH complete lines, not merely to one endpoint: T = translate by (0,+2), H = rotate 180° about K=(0,0), F = reflect over horizontal mirror line h: y=0. Record each transformed line equation, the separation and whether the two images remain parallel.

9.3 — Let's Do Some 180's: vertical angles

Draw lines r: y=0 and s: y=x intersecting at O=(0,0). Label four rays through O and a pair of opposite 45° acute angles; also label the adjacent 135° obtuse angles. Rotate the complete configuration by 180° about O, mapping each ray to its opposite ray. Explain why each acute angle is mapped exactly onto the opposite acute angle and therefore the vertical angles are equal. Can one rigid motion change two parallel lines into intersecting lines? Provide a geometric justification, not just a yes/no choice.
