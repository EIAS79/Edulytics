Calculating Slope

10.1 • Integer and quotient mental exercise

Find pairs a,b with b nonzero such that a/b=−2, a/b=2, and a−b=−2. Give at least two distinct pairs for each.

Explain why each equality is a different constraint and why b=0 is invalid for the first two.

10.2 • General slope formula

Plot (1,11) and (8,2), draw a line and slope triangle, and compute m=(2−11)/(8−1). Explain the negative sign and why flipping both subtraction orders gives the same quotient.

Find an integer x-coordinate for a point on a slope 2 line through (3,5), and one point on slope −2 through (4,1). Justify using exact differences.

10.3 • Private design and blank-grid partner cards

Teacher prints separate confidential line-design cards and empty-grid cards. One student describes the line without revealing the paper; the other plots from the spoken instructions.

Explain what coordinate and slope details suffice to fix a unique line; teacher checks the reconstruction and oral communication.

Independent Practice • Four slope skills

P1: Compute slopes of a line through (−3,4),(4,−3) and a line through (1,11),(8,2).

P2: Match point pairs (0,0),(2,3); (2,5),(6,3); (3,0),(3,4) to their slope classifications.

P3: Draw a slope 4 line through (1,−2) and give another point.

P4: Draw slope 4 with negative y-intercept and write a valid equation.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down • Reverse both differences

The line passes through (−1,8) and (5,−1). Calculate its slope two ways by reversing both subtraction orders.

A peer states m=9/6 because the line slopes down to the right. Explain the sign error.
