2.1 — Notice and Wonder: Concentric Circles

Study this first-party circular grid: the circles share center O and successive radii increase by one unit. Write at least two mathematical observations and two testable questions. Distinguish a point on the circle (boundary) from a point strictly inside the circle. Describe how a ray from O meets each concentric circle at one point when the ray is fixed.

2.2 — A Droplet on the Surface: measure four rays

For an independent construction, use small circle c of radius 2 and large circle d of radius 5, both centered O=(0,0). Plot four small-circle points A(2,0), B(0,2), C(-2,0), D(0,-2). Draw a distinct ray from O through each point and extend it to the larger circle; mark corresponding A′,B′,C′,D′. Record small S, large L and quotient L/S for each label, identify the single scale factor, and explain why direction does not change the factor. 2.3 — Quadrilateral on a Circular Grid

Construct asymmetric quadrilateral A(1,0),B(2,1),C(1,3),D(-1,1) and center O=(0,0). Independently construct TWO images of the ORIGINAL quadrilateral: one dilation factor 2 and one dilation factor ½. Label every image vertex A′...D′ and A″...D″ and connect them in corresponding order. Also take point E halfway along original edge AB, find its two images, and explain why all edge points follow the same rule. Compare corresponding side lengths and angles. Extension: for a segment not passing through O, discuss whether its doubled image length must be twice the original (yes), whether it must be five units longer (not necessarily), and whether segment and image must intersect (not necessarily).

Independent Edulytiks Practice — four original source skill families

P1 — Image and preimage on two circles: Center O=(0,0), radius of c=2, radius of d=6. Point P=(0,2) on c: find P′ on d. Point Q=(6,0) on d: find its preimage Q₀ on c. Plot both rays and give k and its reciprocal.

P2 — Double and halve a triangle: On a circular/square grid with center O=(0,0), triangle A(2,0),B(0,2),C(2,2) is dilated separately by factor 2 and ½. Give all vertex coordinates, calculate the longest side in the original and two images, and compare the 45°,45°,90° angle pattern. Explain why size changes but angle measures do not.

P3 — Prior-unit rigid congruence: Triangle P(1,1),Q(3,1),R(1,2) maps by translation (+3,-2) to P′,Q′,R′. Draw and label the image and justify why this move makes congruent, not enlarged figures.

P4 — Prior-unit triangle angle retrieval: Three proposed interior angles 38°,98°,44° are given. Do they form a triangle? Explain with the exact angle sum. Then change the last angle to 43° and explain why the revised triple is impossible.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down — two concentric circles and images

On the same grid, circles of radius 2 and 4 share O. Original points A(0,2) and B(2,0) lie on smaller circle. Dilate by factor 2 to the larger circle, draw original and image points along rays OA,OB, calculate both original/image radial distances and justify why O stays fixed.
