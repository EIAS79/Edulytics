4.2 — Make That Move: give reproducible directions

Start with asymmetric quadrilateral A(1,1), B(4,1), C(3,3), D(1,2). Privately choose one of three different first-party movements: (i) translate by (+2,0), (ii) rotate 180° about O=(0,0), or (iii) reflect across the y-axis. Without telling the category first, describe exact movement parameters to a partner, who draws all four corresponding vertices. Switch roles with a different motion. Compare drawings, mark the common vector, center+angle, or mirror line, and explain whether the order of vertices is reversed.

4.3 — A to B to C: two-step composition

Take original triangle A(1,1), B(3,1), C(2,3). First shift right 2, then reflect over the line x=0. Record coordinates after step 1 and step 2, draw corresponding triangles and say which move is applied first. Now perform the reverse order (reflect first, shift right 2 second) on the original. Determine whether the final image is the same; justify by at least one corresponding vertex.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down — What Does It Take?

A separate first-party challenge: triangle P(1,1), Q(3,1), R(2,3) is translated (+2,0) and then reflected over the y-axis. Provide all three final vertices, sketch the two intermediate stages, and state what happens if you reverse the two moves. Explain why lengths and angles remain unchanged and why the final coordinates may differ.

Collect: teacher-flash reconstruction, two partner rounds with complete instructions, two ordered compositions, and original Edulytiks Cool-down. Correct diagrams, alternative precise oral descriptions and teacher notes can all be evidence; a choice letter alone is insufficient. No online scoring, submission or grade persistence is claimed.
