Side Length Quotients in Similar Triangles

9.1 • Compare two side triples

Consider a triangle with sides 2,3,4 and another with sides 4,5,6. Write all three corresponding ratios and explain why one matching ratio does not guarantee similarity.

Contrast a true scale copy: sides 3,4,5 compared with 6,8,10. Label correspondence and factor.

9.2 • Quotients within scaled triangles

Use a first-party triangle with sides 4,5,7. Produce its factor 2, factor 3, and factor 1/2 triangles. Complete a table of three side lengths for each.

For every triangle compare long/short, long/medium, and medium/short quotients. Explain why their values remain unchanged under dilation.

9.3 • Missing side lengths

ABC has side lengths AB=6, BC=9, AC=12. Similar DEF has DE=10, with A↔D, B↔E and C↔F. Find EF and DF.

One classmate proposes EF/BC=AB/DE. Explain the wrong quotient direction and write a consistent proportion.

Independent practice: source skill families

P1: A 3,4,5 triangle is dilated by factor 3/2; find all three image sides.

P2: Construct a 4,5,7 triangle and a 1/4-scale copy. Explain how its angles and the long/medium ratio compare.

P3: The source has corresponding sides 4 and 10, while an image has sides 6 and x. Find x with justified correspondence.

P4: Dilate triangle (0,0),(6,0),(0,9) about the origin by factor 1/3; draw and label its image.
