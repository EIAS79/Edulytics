10.1 • Equal quotients

Write at least three equivalent exact expressions for 15÷12, including a simplified fraction and decimal. Explain why all have the same value.

Discuss why 15/12 and 12/15 do not represent the same orientation.

10.2 • Similar slope triangles on one line

On a coordinate grid draw the line through (0,0),(4,3),(8,6). Build a right slope triangle from (0,0) to (4,3), and a second from (0,0) to (8,6).

Prove the two triangles similar using a right angle plus the line angle. Evaluate the two rise/run quotients and explain equal slope.

10.3 • Multiple lines with one slope

Draw lines y=(3/4)x, y=(3/4)x+2, and y=(3/4)x−3. Mark intercepts.

Compare their direction and explain why they are distinct parallel lines, not congruent line segments.

10.4 • Distinguish slope types

Sketch slope 1/2, slope −2, a horizontal line, and a vertical line. Give two integer points on each and compute rise/run when defined.

Explain in words why a vertical line has undefined slope rather than slope zero.

Independent practice: slopes on square grids

P1: Match lines from (0,0) to (1,1), (2,4), and (5,1) with slopes 1, 2, and 1/5.

P2: Draw three distinct lines of slope 2 and three of slope 1/3. Describe their shared and differing properties.

P3: A line rises 6 when run increases 4 and rises 9 when run increases 6. Explain why the resulting slope triangles are similar.

P4: Explain why side triples 3,4,5 and 6,7,8 are not similar despite both being valid triangles.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down • Undefined is not zero

Line A passes through (−2,1) and (2,7). Calculate its slope and draw the slope triangle.

Line B passes through (3,−1) and (3,5). Calculate its run and explain why the slope is undefined. Contrast with horizontal y=5.
