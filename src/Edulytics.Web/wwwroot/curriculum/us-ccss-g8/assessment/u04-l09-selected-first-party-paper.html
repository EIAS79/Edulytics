9.1 Which Would You Choose?

Babysitting plan A pays $15 starting bonus plus $8 per hour; plan B pays $10 per hour. Write earnings equations and solve for equal pay.

Explain why equality occurs after 7.5 paid hours, and whether half-hour payments are allowed by the employer.

9.2 Water Tanks

Tank A holds 70 liters initially and drains at 3 liters/minute; B holds 20 liters initially and fills at 2 liters/minute. Record both volumes at t=0,5,10,15.

Solve the equation for equal amounts; assess nonnegative water and realistic capacity.

9.3 Elevators

Elevator A starts at floor2 ascending 2 floors/minute, B at floor12 descending 1 floor/minute. Model positions and find their meeting time and theoretical floor.

Explain why the real-valued mathematical intersection need not correspond to an actual full-minute stop at an integer floor.
