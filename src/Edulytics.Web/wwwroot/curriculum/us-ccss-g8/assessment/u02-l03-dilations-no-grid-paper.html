3.1 — Points on a Ray

First-party model ray starts at A=(0,0) and passes through B=(2,0), with AB length 2 cm. Draw and label C twice as far from A as B, and D half as far. C must be beyond B, D must lie between A and B. On your ungridded drawing label AC, AD and establish why positive dilation points stay on ray AB, not its backward extension.

3.2 — Dilation Obstacle Course

Use this independent one-dimensional labelled configuration (units are consistent, but the task requires explaining each center, not guessing): A=(-5,0), B=(-3,0), F=(-1,0), H=(0,0), E=(2,0), G=(6,0), C=(5,0). Perform every operation, draw the rays and state factor, image and center. (a) Dilate B about A by factor 5: does it reach labelled C? (b) From H, dilate G onto E; then E onto G. (c) Give a center and positive factor mapping F to B. (d) Dilate H about A with factor 1/3, giving exact new point J. (e) Describe a dilation using a labelled center mapping F onto H. (f) With B as center, what factor leaves H fixed? Do not confuse forward and inverse factors.

3.3 — Getting Perspective without a Grid

On blank paper choose C=(0,0) as a labeled reference origin only, not a supplied grid. Using straightedge, draw CP and CQ for P=(2,1) and Q=(4,0); locate P′ for factor 4 and Q′ for factor 1/2, each using the SAME center C. Verify distance ratios and rays. Next draw original triangle T=(1,1),U=(3,1),V=(2,2) with distinct chosen center K=(-1,0), then construct its factor 3/2 image by marking all three center-to-vertex rays and connecting T′U′V′. Draw segments TT′, UU′, VV′ for a perspective sketch. Explain whether the new image lies closer to K (for factor below 1) or farther (for factor above 1), and whether the shape is similar or congruent.

Independent Edulytiks Practice — six source assessment families

P1 — Segment scaling: AB=3 cm. Find image length for factors 5, 3.7, 1/5 and a symbolic positive factor s. Explain why side lengths scale linearly.

P2 — Four point dilations: With A=(0,0),B=(2,0), plot C the factor-2 image of B about A, D the factor-2 image of A about B, E the factor-½ image of B about A and F the factor-½ image of A about B. Explain any coincident results.

P3 — Perspective construction: Draw a different polygon, mark an external center O, choose factor 2 and use individually measured rays to construct the image. Include center, factor, image of every vertex and proof of shared ratios.

P4 — Scale factor in both directions: Similar triangles DEF and ABC have corresponding longest sides DE=8 cm and AB=12 cm. Give DEF→ABC and ABC→DEF exact factors, and explain reciprocals.

P5 — Vertical angles review: Intersecting lines form an angle of 102°. Give opposite and both adjacent angles and explain the 180° straight angle.

P6 — Congruence review: Original triangle A(0,0),B(3,0),C(0,4) is translated (+2,-1) to DEF. Label D,E,F in order, compute each image side length, and give the three angle measures (90°, an angle about 53.13°, and the remaining angle about 36.87°). Explain why this is congruence, not dilation with factor 2.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down — one dilation of a triangle

On a completely blank area draw triangle A=(1,1),B=(5,1),C=(1,3) and center O=(0,0) using a ruler and consistent unit scale. Construct factor 3/2 dilation of the entire triangle, label A′B′C′, calculate all three center distances and matching side ratios, and describe why angles remain equal.
