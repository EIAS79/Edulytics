20.1 ? Sketch a Sphere

Sketch a sphere, label radius and diameter, and explain why radius is the single linear dimension in V=(4/3)?r?.

20.2 ? A Sphere in a Cylinder

Compare a sphere of radius r with a cylinder of radius r and height 2r. Calculate both volumes symbolically.

20.3 ? Spheres in Cylinders

For r=3, calculate sphere, cylinder (h=6), and cone (h=6) volumes. Identify the relationship among them.
