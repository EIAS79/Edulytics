19.1 ? Notice and Wonder: Two Shapes

Compare a hemisphere and familiar right solids with the same radius. List features useful for a volume bound.

19.2 ? Hemispheres in Boxes

Place a hemisphere conceptually inside a cylinder of radius r and height r. Explain why the cylinder gives an upper bound.

19.3 ? Estimating Hemispheres

For r=3, compare cone volume 9?, a reasonable hemisphere estimate between the bounds, and cylinder volume 27?. Explain why bounding is not the same as proving an exact formula.

Independent aligned practice (5 source problems reviewed)

For general r, compare coefficients of ?r? for cone with h=r, hemisphere, and cylinder with h=r.

State one source of error in a numerical fill/measurement experiment.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down

For radius 3, show that a hemisphere volume of 18pi lies between a cone volume of 9pi and cylinder volume of 27pi, and explain what the bounds mean.

Show enough work for a teacher to verify the reasoning, not only the final answer.
