6.1 Equal Perimeters

A triangle has three equal sides of length x+2 and a square has side length 8. Solve 3(x+2)=32. Check both perimeters and x+2>0.

6.2 Predicting Solutions

Predict sign or solution count before solving 4x+2=10, 3x+7=1, 6x=0 and 2(x+3)=2x+6.

Distinguish an identity from one zero solution.

6.3 Which Would You Rather Solve?

Solve 4(x+3)=28 by first dividing or first distributing; compare efficiency.

Classify 5(x−2)=5x−10 and 4(x+1)=4x−2 as all versus no solution.

Four practice families

P1: Solve 2(x−5)=18 and 3x+4=x−8.

P2: Solve (x+1)/3=2x−1.

P3: Critique the claim that 9x+15=3x+15 has no solution; test x=0.

P4: A proportional table has (2,7),(6,21); write its y=kx equation.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down
