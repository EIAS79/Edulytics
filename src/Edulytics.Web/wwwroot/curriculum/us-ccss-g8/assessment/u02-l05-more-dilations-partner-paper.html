Independent Edulytiks Practice — four source skill families

P1 — Image coordinates from given image point: Original quadrilateral A(2,0),B(4,0),C(4,2),D(2,2) is dilated about O=(0,0) so B′=(6,0). Determine k and draw A′B′C′D′. Explain why one corresponding non-center point determines a uniform positive factor.

P2 — Identify center and reciprocal factors: Triangle A has vertices (1,1),(3,1),(1,3). Triangle B has vertices (2,2),(6,2),(2,6); triangle C has vertices (½,½),(3/2,½),(½,3/2). Explain and draw center O. Find A→B, B→A, and C→B factors. Why are these not translations?

P3 — Three relative scalings: Original triangle ABC has vertices (2,0),(0,2),(2,2). Construct images at factor 2 and ½, each about O=(0,0). Can the factor-½ image be produced from the factor-2 image? State the relative factor and center and explain why ¼, not ½, is required.

P4 — Right-triangle angle retrieval: Right triangle DEF has D=28°. Find both other angles and justify which is 90° and which is 62°. State why the order of the remaining labels must be supported by the original right-angle mark if it is not specified.
