Teacher cuts and distributes one card per student. On your card, sketch the line through origin on axes with the stated tick spacing, then find two coordinate pairs.

Find other cards with the SAME mathematical relationship (not the same paper angle). Prove groups by labeled quotient and explain disagreements.

2.3 • Distinguish a changing tank from constant flow

Tank A has volumes 0,4,11,15 liters after 0,1,2,3 min. Tank B gains exactly5 liters each minute and starts empty. Graph both, using two different axes scales.

Which relation is proportional? Show constant versus unequal differences between adjacent one-minute intervals.

Independent Practice • Four mathematical roles

P1: A hare travels16 miles as a tortoise travels4 miles over equal time. Compare the rates.

P2: A spring extends3cm for2N and9cm for6N; verify constant extension per newton.

P3: Draw 2×4 and4×8 rectangles, then describe a sequence of rigid moves and a dilation mapping the small to large.

P4: A classmate says congruent figures cannot be similar. Refute with scale factor1.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down • Recognize identical rates

Line A goes through origin and (4,10); line B through origin and (8,20), drawn with unequal tick marks. Are the relationships identical? Show exact rates.

Explain why a line's drawn angle without axis units does not prove its real-world rate.
