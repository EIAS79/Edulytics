16.1 ? Represent distance remaining

A car begins 4 miles from home and travels toward home at 0.5 mile per minute. Choose and justify an expression for distance remaining after t minutes.

State the meaningful time interval for the expression and explain the sign of the rate.

16.2 ? Solve, create, exchange, and critique systems

Two cyclists start 7 miles apart at 8:00 and ride toward each other at 0.20 and 0.15 mile per minute. Determine the meeting time and verify the combined-distance model.

A fundraiser sells $1 fruit items and $10 nut bags. If 100 items bring in $307, determine both quantities and check total count and revenue.

One worker earns $7 per hour. Another earns $5 for the first hour and $8 for each additional hour. Find when their earnings match and state the valid domain.

Invent a new two-constraint context with an integer solution. Exchange it with a partner, solve independently, and critique whether the equations and interpretation match the story.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down ? Context, system, and verification

A venue sold 40 tickets. Adult tickets cost $8 and youth tickets cost $5. Total revenue was $260. Write a system and solve for both ticket counts.

Verify the counts and revenue, include units, and explain why the ordered pair is the unique solution.
