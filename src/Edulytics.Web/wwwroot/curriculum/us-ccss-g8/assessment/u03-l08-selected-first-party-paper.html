8.1 • Which graphed lines are translations?

Plot f:y=2x−1, h:y=2x+5, i:y=2x−4, g:y=−2x+5 and j:y=x+5 on one coordinate grid.

Identify which lines are translations of f. Explain why pure translations preserve slope, even when vertical intercept changes.

8.2 • Increased savings

Diego earns $10 per hour and begins with $0. Write y=10h. Suppose he begins with $25 in savings instead: write the new equation.

Create tables and graphs for both. Explain why rate10 remains unchanged and the starting amount changes the y-intercept.

8.3 • Translating a line from points

Place point A at (0,2), (0,5), and (0,−3); draw the line with slope 3/2 through each A.

For a rightward horizontal translation of y=(3/2)x+2 by 4 units, derive the transformed equation y=(3/2)(x−4)+2. Explain why this is not a vertical translation.

P3: Cable fee is $70 monthly with a one-time $30 installation charge. Model total after m months and identify slope/intercept.

P5: Match a zero-intercept proportional line and a positive-intercept affine line to two independent contexts.
