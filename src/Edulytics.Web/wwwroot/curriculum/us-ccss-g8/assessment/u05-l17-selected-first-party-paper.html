17.2 ? Double the Edge

For a rectangular prism, double only one edge while holding the other two fixed. Predict and verify the volume factor.

17.3 ? Halve the Height

For a cylinder with fixed radius, halve the height. State the effect on volume and show it algebraically.

With cone radius fixed, write volume as a function of height and graph several points. Explain why it is linear in h.
