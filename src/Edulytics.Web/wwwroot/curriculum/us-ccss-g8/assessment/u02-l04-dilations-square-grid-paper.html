Independent Edulytiks Practice — all five source families

P1 — Noncongruence by dilation: Triangle ABC dilated about an external center D by factor 2 has all corresponding angles equal. Is it congruent? Explain why equal angles alone do not establish congruence; use side-length scaling.

P2 — Three independently centered quadrilateral images: P=(7,6), Q=(5,4), R=(9,3), S=(11,5), O=(5,1). Construct (a) k2 about R, (b) k½ about O, and (c) k½ about S. Give every labelled corresponding vertex for all three; do NOT apply one image to create the next.

P3 — Nonparallel transversal angle review: Two distinct intersections each involve a slanted transversal, but the crossed lines are not stated parallel. At the first crossing an angle is 35°; at the second crossing one angle is 27°. At EACH crossing give the vertically opposite and adjacent supplementary measures. Explain why corresponding 35° and 27° angles must NOT be falsely set equal by a parallel-lines theorem.

P4 — Rigid-motion sequence review: Polygon P is triangle (0,0),(2,0),(0,1). Reflect over the y-axis and then translate by (+3,+1). Draw resulting polygon Q, show all three resulting image vertices, and justify how those two rigid motions preserve lengths and angles. A different valid rigid sequence may be explained if it gives exactly Q.

P5 — Ordered transformation review: Start B=(−2,−5), translate down 4, reflect across the y-axis, then translate up 6. Give EVERY intermediate point and final image. Describe why changing the order of reflection and translation can affect the result.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down — find the center-specific image

With center C=(2,1) and triangle A=(4,1), B=(4,3), D=(2,3), dilate at factor 3/2. Draw all three rays from C, give the three exact image vertices and check that all corresponding triangle side lengths scale by 3/2 while angles are unchanged. Do not multiply raw global coordinates by 3/2. This is a distinct first-party task; the publisher 4.4 Cool-down is not reproduced.
