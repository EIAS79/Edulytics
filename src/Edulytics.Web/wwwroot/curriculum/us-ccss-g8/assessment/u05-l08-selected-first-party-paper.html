Linear Functions

8.1 ? Bigger and Smaller

Compare y=3x+2 and y=5x-4 at several x-values. Identify which has larger rate and how initial values affect early outputs.

8.2 ? Proportional Relationships Define Linear Functions

Explain why y=4x is both proportional and linear, and why y=4x+7 is linear but not proportional.

8.3 ? Is it Filling Up or Draining Out?

A tank has 40 L and drains at 2.5 L/min. Write a linear function, identify slope/intercept, and state a meaningful domain.

8.4 ? Which is Growing Faster?

Use two tables with equal x-steps to compare constant differences and decide which linear function has greater slope.
