3.1 — Notice and Wonder: The Isometric Grid

Observe the first-party triangular grid model: at each marked grid intersection six equal triangular sectors meet. Describe what you notice about the repeated line directions and equal edge lengths. What changes when you use it instead of square coordinate paper? Sketch three rays at 0°, 60°, and 120° through O, identify two equal-length nonperpendicular basis directions, and explain why the axes are not 90° apart.

3.2 — Transformation Information on square and isometric grids

Make three independent labelled images of the original, not a cumulative composition: (T) translate 2 right and 1 down; (R) rotate 90° counterclockwise about O=(0,0); (F) reflect across the y-axis. For each image show A′, B′, C′ and give their coordinates. Mark a corresponding side and explain why its length stays the same.
