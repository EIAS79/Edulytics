11.1 • Notice and Wonder about Two Moving Bugs

Draw two insect routes 12 cm apart. One starts at position 0 at 2 cm/sec, the other starts at 12 cm and moves toward the first at 1 cm/sec.

Write observations and a prediction of when they meet before solving.

11.2 • Bugs Passing in the Night, Continued

Use the two movement models p=2t and p=12−t; construct tables and graphs and solve simultaneous equations.

Explain why the crossing is a common position and time, and verify the pair in both rules.

11.3 • A Close Race

Two cyclists start a 100-m race together. A travels at 5 m/s while B travels at 4 m/s. Graph time versus distance.

Find finish times, compare relative lead at 10 sec, and explain whether their graph lines intersect again after start.

Four practice families

P1: Diego has $11 and saves $5/week; Lin has $60 and spends $2/week. Find when their balances are equal.

P2: Find intersection of y=−2x/3+3 and y=2x−5.

P3: A line has slope−3 and passes through an intersection with y=2; one equation is y=−3x+5. Write another line with slope1 through the same point.

P4: A farm has cows and chickens with 82 total legs. Give two distinct nonnegative whole-number combinations of cows and chickens and explain why one equation alone is not enough for unique counts.

Original Edulytiks Cool-down • Check both equations

Find intersection of y=x+1 and y=−x+5 by algebra and graph.

Check the result in both equations and explain why a point on only one line is not a system solution.
