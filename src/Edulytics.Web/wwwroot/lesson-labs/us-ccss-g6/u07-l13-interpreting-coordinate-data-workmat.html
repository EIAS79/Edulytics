Teacher rubric: (-2,-4) inIII, (0,1) on y-axis, (2,5) inI; x=0 noon. Teacher checks axis names, signed scales and point readings. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
