Median as Middle of Sorted Data

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

13.1–13.3 • Find the middle

Sort ten game scores:120,120,130,130,140,150,160,160,170,190. With an even count, median is the average of fifth and sixth values:145.

Median versus mean

The same scores total1470, so mean147. Compare what each statistic tells you and why a middle can differ from fair-share.
