Original practice — unit conversions and fractions

A cube edge one-third cm has volume (1/3)³=1/27 cm³, not a rounded decimal. A cube with volume 8ft³ has edge 2ft. Original practice 5: Elena calls a 12in×12in face 144 square feet. This is incorrect: 144in² = 1ft²; a 1ft×1ft×2ft box has total outside area 10ft², not 296ft².
