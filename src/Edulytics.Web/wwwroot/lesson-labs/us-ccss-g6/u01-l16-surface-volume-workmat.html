Original 16.2 — build two shapes with 8 unit cubes each

Start with sixteen cubes. Build two connected solids, each with exactly eight cubes. Examples for independent checking: compact 2×2×2 has surface area24; 1×2×4 has28; 1×1×8 has34. Explain how internal face contacts reduce exposed area, even while every model holds eight cubic units.

Original practice pictured four cubes 1×1×4 have volume4 and exposed area18. Doubling the number of cubes does not necessarily double the outside area.
