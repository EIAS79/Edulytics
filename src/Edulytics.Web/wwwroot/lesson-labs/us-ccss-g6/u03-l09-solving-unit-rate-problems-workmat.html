Solving Rate Problems

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

9.1–9.2 • Choose the per-one quotient

Rate compares two distinct quantities. To decide the best deal, divide package cost by count, preserving cents per item. When comparing performance, divide output by elapsed time.
