How Much in One Group?

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

8.1 • Find the size of one full group

One cup of strawberries is¾ of the quantity for one cake. Divide1 by¾ to obtain1⅓ cups for a complete cake. Explain what the divisor and result represent.

8.2 • Fractional recipe tapes

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Three quarters of a liter of juice fill one-half of a container. How much juice can the full container hold? Use an equation and a tape diagram.
