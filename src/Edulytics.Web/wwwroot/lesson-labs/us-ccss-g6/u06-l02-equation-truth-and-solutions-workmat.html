Truth and Equations

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

2.1 • Is the equation true?

Decide whether each equality holds:5+0=0;15×0=0;1.4+2.7=4.1. A variable is a symbol for a quantity, and a solution is a value that makes its equation true.
