The Burj Khalifa

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

1.1 • Estimate a large height

Use an explicit model such as a 2-meter-tall person beside a building silhouette. Estimate the number of person-heights that might fit; record assumptions and explain why this is not a measured building height.

1.2 • Window washing at an assumed constant rate

Suppose15 windows take18 minutes to clean. In a simplified historical scenario involving24,348 windows, estimate active washing time using1.2 minutes/window. Explain the reciprocal rate of5/6 window per minute and why workers' breaks and window sizes matter.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

A lift travels240 feet in8 seconds at a constant modeled speed. Find its speed in feet per second and the predicted distance in15 seconds. Explain why actual acceleration might alter the result.
