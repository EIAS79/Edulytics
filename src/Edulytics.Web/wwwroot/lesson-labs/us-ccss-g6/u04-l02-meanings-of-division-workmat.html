2.2 • Diagram the missing factor

Use a tape of20 equal pound-units. Show five groups with four units, then four groups with five units. Label what each block represents. Discuss why the numerical quotient is equal while the answer units differ.

Interpret fractional groups

Distribute2½ gallons into5 equal bottles to get½ gallon each, and check by multiplication5×½=2½. Draw fractional blocks to explain the division before writing only a number.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Twenty-four students go on a field trip. If there are6 equal groups, how many students are in each? If each group holds6 students, how many groups? Draw both groupings and label the answers.

Teacher rubric: Both numerical results4, but first is4students/group and second4groups. Teacher checks the two diagrams, question meaning and group units.
