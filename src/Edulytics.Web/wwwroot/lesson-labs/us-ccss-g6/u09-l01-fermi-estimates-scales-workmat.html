Estimating Large Quantities with Fermi Problems

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

1.1 • Ant Trek

Estimate how long an ant moving18mm per second without stops would take to travel roughly3944km. Convert units, calculate an approximate time in days and years, and list at least three real-world limitations of this model.

1.2 • Cereal warehouse

Design a warehouse10feet high to store an estimated annual amount of cereal boxes. Declare estimated number of boxes and each box's dimensions. Calculate volume, choose possible floor dimensions, then address packing gaps and aisles.

1.3 • Monument covering

Model all external faces, including hidden faces, using trapezoids and other shapes. Estimate surface area, tile area, number of tiles and a waste allowance. Explain why your result is an estimate, not a measured monument specification.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down
