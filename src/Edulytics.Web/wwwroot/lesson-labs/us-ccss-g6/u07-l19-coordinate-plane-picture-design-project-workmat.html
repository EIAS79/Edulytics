Designing Pictures on the Coordinate Plane

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

19.1 • Recreate a picture from ordered pairs

Use the teacher-supplied Cat Pictures source model and a graphing tool to enter the coordinates in their given order. Connect them exactly, checking that both axes have a sensible equal scale. Correct a point only after comparing the plotted result with the provided source.

19.2 • Design an image

Design your own picture by listing at least eight ordered pairs. Example independent guide points: (-4,-2),(-4,2),(-2,4),(0,2),(2,4),(4,2),(4,-2),(-4,-2). Connect them and revise the design. Explain which points are mirrored across the y-axis.

Your image does not have to match this example; the original project is creative and assessed by a teacher. This independent model is not a publisher artwork answer.

Optional numeric check

As a separate arithmetic check, plot(-2,-1),(-2,3),(2,3),(2,-1). Explain why its side lengths are4, area16 square units and perimeter16 units. The numeric check does not replace the original graphing capstone.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Independent optional reflection: Create a rectangle from(-2,-1),(-2,3),(2,3),(2,-1), closing the loop. Explain its area/perimeter and how ordered pairs instruct a plotter.

Teacher rubric: Four-unit sides, area16 square units and perimeter16 units. Teacher evaluates plotted sequence, axis labels, coordinates and the written explanation. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
