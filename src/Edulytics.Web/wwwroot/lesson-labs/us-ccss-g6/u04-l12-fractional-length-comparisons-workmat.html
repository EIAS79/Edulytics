Fractional Lengths and Comparisons

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

12.1–12.2 • Compare lengths multiplicatively

Find how many times as tall a9⅜ft giraffe is as a6¼ft zookeeper. Divide two heights measured in the same units to get1½. Reverse the order to find2/3.
