Using Decimal Operations to Make Shopping Decisions

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

1.1 • Snack-bar choices

Mai starts with$14.50, spends$4.35 at the concession stand and$5.25 at the arcade. Estimate first, then calculate the remaining$4.90 using aligned decimal places or integer cents.

Consider whole items and change

Each pizza costs$7.50 and the budget is$40. Five whole pizzas are affordable; six are not. Show why a decimal quotient does not automatically mean a fraction of a pizza can be purchased as one whole pizza.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

A dinner order includes2 soups at$3.75 each and3 drinks at$1.40 each. Estimate, calculate the exact bill and change from$15.
