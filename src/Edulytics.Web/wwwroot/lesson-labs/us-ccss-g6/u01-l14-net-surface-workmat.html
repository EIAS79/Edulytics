Independent Edulytics Cool-down — not publisher 14.3

Construct a valid net of a 4×3×2 rectangular prism with all six faces exactly once. Label both 4×3 faces, 4×2 faces and 3×2 faces. Find the area 52 square units and explain how you confirmed the net folds without overlapping faces.

Teacher rubric: all six required rectangles present in opposite pairs; a net sharing complete folding edges; correctly matched edge lengths, no overlap or gap when folded; complete area calculation with square units; comparison with a different valid arrangement and a written reason. Grade drawings and folding separately from numeric checks.
