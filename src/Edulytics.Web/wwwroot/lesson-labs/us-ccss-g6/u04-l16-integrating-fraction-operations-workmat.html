Operations with Fractions

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

16.1 • Match operations with questions

To find orange count, divide2½ cups by¼ cup per orange, giving10. To combine different weights, add. To find 'how much heavier,' subtract.

16.2 • Interpreting a hiking sign

DistanceMiles
Already walked¾
Remaining1½
Full trip2¼

Explain why already walked is one-third, remaining is twice already walked, and total is triple already walked.

16.3–16.4 • Use operations coherently

Compare cat5½lb to dog8¼lb through division, addition and subtraction. Find80 ice cubes from a7½×6×6ft prism and1½ft block edges. For a ratio2 pennies:3 dimes, explain why coin count is unknown until total55 is supplied.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

A box holds3¾ dozen pencils. Each work table requires¾ dozen. How many complete tables can be supplied? Draw equal fractional groups and check with multiplication.

Teacher rubric: (15/4)÷(3/4)=5 tables,5×3/4=15/4 dozen. Teacher checks unit dozen and equal groups. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
