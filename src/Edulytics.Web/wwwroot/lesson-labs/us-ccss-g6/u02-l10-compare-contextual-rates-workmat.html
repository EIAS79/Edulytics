10.2 • Concert or tulip tickets

Use a table or a double number line to compare two sales. Example tulip shops:5 bulbs/$11 versus6 bulbs/$13. Which is the lower dollar price per bulb? Explain the unit clearly.

10.3 • Flavor and color

Compare milkshake mixtures2 scoops:1 cup and3 scoops:2 cups. Which has more chocolate per cup? Compare purple mixes5 purple:2 white against15 purple:8 white. Explain why a change in one ingredient changes concentration.
