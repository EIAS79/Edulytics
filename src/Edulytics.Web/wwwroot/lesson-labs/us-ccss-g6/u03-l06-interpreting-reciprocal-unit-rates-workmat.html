Interpreting Rates

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

6.1 • Two useful rates

At12 dollars for3 pints, calculate4 dollars per pint and¼ pint per dollar. These are reciprocal numbers but answer different questions.

QuestionRate
What does one pint cost?$4 per pint
How much does one dollar buy?¼ pint per dollar

6.2 • Cooking and painting

Polenta uses5 cups water for2 cups cornmeal. Find cornmeal for3 cups water and water for3 cups cornmeal; explain why using two different reciprocal unit rates is reasonable.

6.3 • Pick the right rate

Paint covers350 square feet per gallon. For1750 square feet, use350 square feet per gallon to find5 gallons. Explain how the other rate,1/350 gallon per square foot, gives the same quantity.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

At a market,3 pounds of grapes cost9 dollars. State the price per pound and the pounds per dollar, then find the quantity purchasable with12 dollars.

Teacher rubric: 3 dollars per pound;1/3 pound per dollar;12 dollars buys4 pounds. Teacher checks which unit is numerator and denominator and why the rates serve different questions.
