Evaluating Expressions with Exponents

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

14.1 • Exponents before operations

8×3²=8×9=72, but(8×3)²=24²=576. Parentheses determine which quantity is squared.

14.2 • Surface area and volume

For ten separate cubes each5in per side, total volume is10×5³=1250 cubic inches. Their total surface area, counting faces of separate cubes, is10×6×5²=1500 square inches. Discuss what changes if cubes touch.
