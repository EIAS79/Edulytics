15.1–15.2 • A variable can be the base or exponent

When x=3,2^x=8 but x²=9. Parentheses matter:3x² means3 times x squared, not(3x)².

15.3 • Find a value making an equation true

EquationPositive solution
7+x²=16x=3
5-x²=1x=2
2×2³=2^xx=4
3⁴/3^x=27x=1

Substitute into the full equation to verify. Other real solutions may exist for squaring equations, but the classroom choices here are positive.
