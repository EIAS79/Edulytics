Constructing the Coordinate Plane

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

12.1–12.2 • Choose an axis scale

Use fifths for fractional pairs(⅕,⅘),(-⅗,⅖),(-1⅕,-⅘),(⅕,-⅗). Mark axes in equal fifth-unit steps to place points precisely.

12.2 • Scale larger data

For coordinates(-50,0),(150,100),(200,-100),(350,50),(-250,0), a50-unit tick scale is useful. Draw at least-250 to350 on x and-100 to100 on y, labeling each step.

12.3 • A square centered at zero

Plot(-3,-3),(-3,3),(3,3),(3,-3) to form a square with its center at the origin. Explain why opposite x and y distances imply symmetry, and show any alternative valid centered square.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Plot the points(-1/2,3/2) and(1,-1) using an appropriately labeled coordinate grid. Identify quadrants, explain where the origin belongs and justify your tick scale.

Teacher rubric: First point in II and second in IV; ticks of1/2 make both precise; origin(0,0) on both axes. Teacher checks grid scale, axes and explanations.
