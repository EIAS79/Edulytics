Points on the Coordinate Plane

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

11.1 • Guess my line

Mark a line through(-3.5,4),(0,4),(6.2,4). All points have y=4, so the line is horizontal. Give two more valid points and check their y values.

11.2 • Four quadrants

PointQuadrant
(-2,3)II
(2,3)I
(-2,-3)III
(2,-3)IV

Connect the points around the perimeter to make a rectangle. Label x and y axes, using the x-coordinate before the y-coordinate.

11.3 • Target locations

Draw a coordinate target centered at(0,0). Plot(-4,2),(3,-1) and(0,-5). Explain why the point on the y-axis has no quadrant even though its y-coordinate is negative.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Plot and label(-4,2),(3,-1),(0,-5). State the quadrant of the first two points and why the third point is on an axis rather than in a quadrant.

Teacher rubric: (-4,2) in quadrant II; (3,-1) in quadrant IV; (0,-5) on negative y-axis. Teacher verifies an accurately scaled grid, origin, coordinate order and quadrant labels. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
