Measuring with Different-Sized Units

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

3.1 • Estimate before measuring

Measure the width of a piece of paper with a ruler. Predict in centimeters and inches, record the actual measurements and explain why the values differ while the paper stays the same size.

3.2 • Four measurement stations

Station A: length of paper in centimeters and inches. Station B: room length in meters and yards. Station C: water capacity in liters and milliliters. Station D: small object's mass in grams and kilograms. Record each estimated and actual value with units.

Check approximate conversions

A person57 inches tall is roughly144.8cm; a20-quart pot holds roughly19liters;4½lb is roughly2.04kg. These approximate conversions have different attributes and are not interchangeable.
