Relationships in Real-World Situations

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

19.1 • Match tables and equations

Your teacher provides the original nine equation-table pairs. For each, test several rows by substituting the inputs into the expression, and explain which quantities the variables represent. Do not assume that every relationship is proportional.

19.3 • Graph and teach

Create a correctly scaled graph of your assigned original equation. Label both axes with quantities and units, interpret at least two plotted points, and explain the equation to a partner. Revise after feedback for teacher assessment.
