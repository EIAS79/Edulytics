Equivalent Expressions

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

8.1–8.2 • Equal once is not equivalent

x+3 and2x both equal6 when x=3, but differ when x=1. Therefore they are not equivalent for every value.
