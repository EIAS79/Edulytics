How Many Groups of Fractions?

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

5.1 • A fractional number of groups

To determine how many groups of⅓ are in½, express both in sixths:½=3/6 and⅓=2/6. Then three sixths make1½ groups of two sixths.

5.2 • Pattern blocks and the whole

When using the red trapezoid as the reference whole in one activity, redraw a known part relative to that whole. Compare to an activity using the yellow hexagon as one whole; the unit of the fraction must be stated.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Three quarters of a kilogram of fruit is packed into portions of half a kilogram. How many half-kilogram portions can the amount make, counting a partially filled portion? Show the fraction of the last portion.

Teacher rubric: 3/4÷1/2=3/2=1½ portions. Teacher checks consistent weight units and labeled fractional part of a portion. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
