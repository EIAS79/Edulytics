Paper Boxes: Measuring and Comparing

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

15.1 • Measure and predict

Use three actual square paper sheets; record each side length to the nearest millimeter before folding. Compare their ratios. The original classroom suggests example paper sheets around6,8,12 inches, but use your measured values rather than assumed dimensions.

15.2 • Fold, measure and compare

After folding open-top square-base boxes, measure box length and height and calculate actual measured surface area: base area plus four side faces. Record all measurements and units in a table and explain likely measurement uncertainty.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Optional independent reflection: If all lengths of an open-top model box double, why does its surface area become four times as large rather than twice? What would happen to its volume?

Teacher rubric: Area scales by2²=4 and volume by2³=8. Teacher checks distinction between modeled dimensions and actual measured box variation. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
