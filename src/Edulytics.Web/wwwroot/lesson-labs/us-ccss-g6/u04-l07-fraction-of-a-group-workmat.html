Independent Edulytics Cool-down

A container holds3/4 liter when full. It currently holds1/2 liter. What fraction of its capacity is filled? Show how much of the full group's length is used.

Teacher rubric: (1/2)/(3/4)=2/3 of capacity. Teacher checks consistent liters, whole-capacity reference and a shaded portion representing two thirds. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
