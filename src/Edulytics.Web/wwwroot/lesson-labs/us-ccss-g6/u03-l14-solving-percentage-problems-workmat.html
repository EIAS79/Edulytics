14.2 • Coupons

A20%-off coupon saves13 dollars; find the original price65 dollars. A16-dollar scarf with12-dollar savings gets75% discount. Explain why the saving itself is not automatically the original reference amount.

14.3 • Information gap

Partner A knows a music device's sale price; Partner B knows its percentage discount and original price. Ask only the missing relevant fact. Explain how it supplies the100% reference and compare two methods: a tape diagram or a percent equation.
