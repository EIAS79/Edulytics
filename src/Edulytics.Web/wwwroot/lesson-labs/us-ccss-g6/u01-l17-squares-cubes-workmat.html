Original 17.2 — From 32 cubes to one largest complete cube

One 3-by-3 layer uses 9 unit cubes. Three identical layers make a 3-by-3-by-3 cube with 27 unit cubes; 5 remain from 32. An edge4 cube requires 64 unit cubes, so 32 do not suffice. With an inventory of 64, use all cubes and build the 4-by-4-by-4 cube.
