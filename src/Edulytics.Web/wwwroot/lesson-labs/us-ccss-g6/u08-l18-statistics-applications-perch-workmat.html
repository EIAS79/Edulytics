18.2–18.3 • Homework data stories

Use dot plot, box plot and at least two histograms with different bin widths for the provided original homework assignment data. Compare how each representation communicates common values, gaps and exceptional workloads.

18.4 • Fish populations and uncertainty

Study the provided historical yellow-perch sample data. Describe the histogram, choose mean/MAD or median/IQR with justification, and make a cautious statement about possible population trends. Distinguish sample observations from a guarantee about species survival.
