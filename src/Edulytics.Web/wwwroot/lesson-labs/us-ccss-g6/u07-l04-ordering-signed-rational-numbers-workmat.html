Ordering Rational Numbers

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

4.1–4.2 • Order all rational numbers

Negative numbers lie to the left of zero; among negative numbers, farther left means smaller. In the source, -4 is greater than-5, while -6 is smaller.
