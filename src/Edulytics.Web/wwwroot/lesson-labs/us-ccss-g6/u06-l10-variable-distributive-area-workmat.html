10.1–10.2 • Area with variable widths

A rectangle of height a has widths2,3,4. Add the three areas2a+3a+4a or multiply a by combined width9 to obtain9a.

10.3 • Subtract an unshaded part

For a rectangle height6 total widthm with a2-unit unshaded strip, shaded widthm-2 and area6(m-2). Explain why switching the subtraction order changes the expression.

Different expressions, one area

Draw a rectangle t high and width5+4. It shows5t+4t and9t are equivalent for all t. Contrast with 20t, which does not express the same area.
