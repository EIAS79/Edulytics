13.1–13.2 • Area and fractional side length

Use area=length×width. A rectangle with11m² area and3⅔m side has an unknown side of3m, since3×3⅔=11.

13.4 • Tile geometry vs tile purchase

A12ft by15ft floor has180sqft area. A square tile with side1⅓ft has16/9sqft area. Along the floor's dimensions, count9 tile lengths and11¼ tile widths, then draw where cut tiles are needed. Do not mistake a fractional tile-area count for an exact count of intact tiles purchased.
