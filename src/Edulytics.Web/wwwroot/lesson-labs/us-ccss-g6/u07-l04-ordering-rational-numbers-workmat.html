Ordering Rational Numbers

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

4.1 • Compare rational values

Any positive rational is greater than any negative rational. Negative numbers farther to the left are smaller: -12 < -6 < -5 < -4 < -1 < 0. Mark opposite numbers at equal distance on either side of zero.

4.2 • Order the cards

Sort six cards: ½,0,1,-1½,-½,-1. The correct order is -1½,-1,-½,0,½,1. Explain where each card belongs on a labeled number line, including half-unit positions.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Place -2.5, -1/2, 0, 1.25 and -1.75 in order from least to greatest. Identify the least value and the opposite of -1/2, explaining with number-line position.

Teacher rubric: Order -2.5,-1.75,-0.5,0,1.25; least -2.5; opposite of-1/2 is+1/2. Teacher checks correct positions and comparison language. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
