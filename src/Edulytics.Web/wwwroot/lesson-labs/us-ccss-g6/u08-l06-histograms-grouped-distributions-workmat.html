Histograms and Grouped Numerical Data

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

6.1–6.2 • What histogram bars mean

One bar shows the number of measurements within an interval, not how many have a single exact value. For example, interval8 to less than10 includes8 and9 but excludes10.
