Original Practice 1 — Correct Jada's areas

A triangular prism net has three rectangles of areas 20, 16, and 12 cm², and TWO right triangles each base4cm and perpendicular height3cm. Jada wrote 12 cm² per triangle. Identify her mistake, replace each triangle area with 6 cm², then add all five outside faces for 60 cm². The hypotenuse is 5cm, but it is not the perpendicular height to the 4cm base.

Original Practice 3 — Twelve unit-cube steps

Build a stair shape with three adjacent columns of heights 1,2,3, each TWO cubes deep: total 2×(1+2+3)=12 cubes. All outer square faces total 36. Remove the top two cubes from the highest column, leaving heights 1,2,2; outer area becomes 30. Teachers examine the actual 3D build to ensure all hidden faces are included or excluded correctly.

Edulytics independent Cool-down (NOT original publisher 15.4)

Cut out or draw a foldable net for a right-triangular prism with triangular legs3cm and4cm (hypotenuse5cm) and prism length6cm. Show both congruent triangular faces and all three lateral rectangles. Calculate surface area 84 cm² and write why all five outside polygons are needed.

Teacher rubric: two congruent 3–4–5 right triangles area6 each; three rectangles areas18,24,30; sum84; all shared edges match in the drawn/folded net, no overlaps/gaps; explanation distinguishes area in cm² from prism capacity in cm³. Only the teacher checks the geometry and argument.
