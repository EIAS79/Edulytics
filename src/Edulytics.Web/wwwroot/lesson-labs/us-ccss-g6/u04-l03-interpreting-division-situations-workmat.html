Interpreting Division Situations

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

3.1–3.2 • Different questions, same picture

Arrange16 marks as2 rows with8 marks each. Asking how many rows of8 yields16÷8=2; asking how many per row in2 rows yields16÷2=8. Check either answer by multiplication.

3.3 • Fractions in groups

How many groups of1⅓ are in4? Draw three equal groups or use4÷4/3=3 and check3×4/3=4. Label each whole and third.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Fifteen cups of rice are distributed equally among3 containers. How many cups are in each? If each container instead holds3 cups, how many containers are needed? Draw both meanings.

Teacher rubric: Five cups per container in the first situation, five containers in the second. Teacher checks unknown-factor meaning and group labels. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
