Developing an Algorithm for Fraction Division

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

10.1 • Whole-number division

24 apples split into4-apple groups yields6 groups. If groups hold½ apple each, there are48 groups instead. Draw the equal-size group bars and show why smaller group sizes produce more groups.

10.2 • Divide by a unit fraction

Each whole contains ten tenths, so4÷1/10=40. Count tenths on a ruler and compare1,4 and18 wholes.

10.3 • Divide by a fraction

To divide by3/10, multiply by10 then divide by3:5÷3/10=50/3. This is the same as multiplying by the reciprocal10/3. Verify using equal groups and multiplication.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Calculate 2÷1/3 and2÷3/5 with reciprocal steps. Explain how both could be represented by fraction strips.

Teacher rubric: 2÷1/3=6,2÷3/5=10/3 (3⅓). Teacher checks sixth/third partitions and count of partial groups. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
