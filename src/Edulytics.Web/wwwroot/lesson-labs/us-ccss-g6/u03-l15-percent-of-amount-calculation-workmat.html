Finding This Percent of That

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

15.1 • Calculate percentages efficiently

Use multiplying by a decimal or a fraction: 40% of75 means0.4×75 or2/5×75, both equal30. This method applies even when the original amount is not easy to divide mentally.

15.3 • Savings and different references

For roll packages, compare the per-roll rate and total for24 rolls: four six-packs cost21.12 dollars, while six four-packs cost20.40. For percents, compare 55% of180,300% of26 and12% of700 by calculating their values, not ordering the percentage labels.
