Solving More Ratio Problems

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

16.1 • Choose the tool

A tape diagram shows ratio parts and totals; a table makes many equivalent pairs; a double number line is effective for repeating intervals. Invent a story for a diagram with3 equal parts of type A and2 of type B.

16.2 • Connect two ratios

Nickels:dimes=2:3 and dimes:quarters=2:5. To combine the ratios, make both dime counts equal:4 nickels,6 dimes,15 quarters. Show25 parts and scale to500 coins.

16.3 • Compare strategies

Two horses gallop32ft/s and28ft/s. Use a table or a double number line to find the gap after5 seconds. Explain why a tape diagram may be more convenient for part-part-whole amounts and a line for speed.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

A party needs3 pizzas for every5 children. If40 children attend, determine the total number of pizzas, draw or tabulate equal groups and explain your chosen representation.

Teacher rubric: 40/5=8 equal groups,3×8=24 pizzas. Teacher checks original group order, labels, unit counts, and a coherent representation-choice explanation. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
