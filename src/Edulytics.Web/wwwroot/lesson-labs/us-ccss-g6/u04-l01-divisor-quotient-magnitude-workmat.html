Size of Divisor and Size of Quotient

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

1.1 • Predict first, then calculate

When the positive divisor is small relative to the dividend, the quotient is large. When positive divisor and dividend are about equal, quotient is about1. Compare4,40,4000 each divided by40.01 without full long division.

1.2 • Stack objects

Consider a one-foot vertical stack made of books, pennies, paper bills or bread slices. Estimate a thickness for each object and predict how many fit in12 inches. Measure a sample rather than assuming all objects have a fixed universal thickness.
