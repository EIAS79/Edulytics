Picking Representatives

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

6.1 • Equal shares of computers

Five families have4,2,6,2,2 children and8 computers. Work out2 children per computer and distribute2,1,3,1,1. With20 children and8 computers, ideal quotas become fractional. Explain how a real allocation must use whole computers.

6.3–6.5 • Allocate seats and draw districts

Schools of48,12,24,36 pupils share10 advisers exactly at12 pupils/adviser:4,1,2,3 seats. For larger uneven populations, show fractional ideal quotas, choose a consistent whole-seat allocation and check minimum representation. On the teacher's color maps, draw equal-area connected districts and explain the change in representation using arithmetic, not advocacy.
