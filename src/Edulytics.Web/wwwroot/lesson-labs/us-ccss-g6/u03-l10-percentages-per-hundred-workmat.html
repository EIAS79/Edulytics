What Are Percentages?

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

10.1 • One dollar is 100 cents

A percent is a rate per100. Four dimes make40cents or40% of a dollar. A quarter plus a nickel make30%; shade30 of100 equal squares to show the fraction.

10.2 • Coin combinations

Create several ways to make30cents and140cents using allowed coins. Explain why the 140% amount requires more than one dollar.

10.3 • Number lines can extend beyond100%

Mark0%,25%,50%,75%,100%,125% and150% on an evenly spaced line and label the corresponding cents0,25,50,75,100,125,150. Distinguish part of one bottle from comparison to someone else's amount.
