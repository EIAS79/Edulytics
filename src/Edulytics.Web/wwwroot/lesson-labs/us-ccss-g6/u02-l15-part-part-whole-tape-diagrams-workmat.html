Part-Part-Whole Ratios

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

15.1–15.2 • Draw ratio units

A yellow:blue paint recipe uses3:2 equal parts. If the total under an additive-volume model is25cups, each part is5cups, making15yellow and10blue. Draw five equal tape blocks with category labels.

15.4 • Invent a problem

Choose a two-category ratio and a whole that is an integer multiple of the sum of the two parts. Write a word problem, diagram, answer and explanation. Exchange problems with a partner for checking.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Two rectangular rooms have an area ratio of3:5. Their combined floor area is64square meters. Find the area of each room and draw a labeled tape diagram.

Teacher rubric: Eight equal ratio parts total64, each8 square meters; smaller room24 and larger40. Check drawing segments, unit square meters and why the areas sum to64. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
