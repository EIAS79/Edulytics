Staying in Balance

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

3.1 • Balanced equations

Three unknown circles each x balance six unit weights. Both hanger and equation show3x=6, so x=2. Removing or dividing equal amounts on both sides preserves balance.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Two equal unknown circle weights balance a known weight of7.2 units. Find the weight of one circle, then draw and label a balanced hanger and inverse check.

Teacher rubric: 2x=7.2 givesx=3.6,2×3.6=7.2. Teacher checks two equal circles, known opposing weight and balanced labels. Automatic Practice checks selected numbers only. Teacher verifies learners' diagrams, scale, annotations, units and verbal justifications separately.
