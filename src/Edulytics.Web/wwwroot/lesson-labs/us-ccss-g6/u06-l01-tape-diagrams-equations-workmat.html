Tape Diagrams and Equations

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

1.1–1.2 • The meaning of a tape

An additive tape for x+4=17 has two adjoining pieces (x and4), together17. A multiplicative tape for5x=35 has five equal pieces, each x.
