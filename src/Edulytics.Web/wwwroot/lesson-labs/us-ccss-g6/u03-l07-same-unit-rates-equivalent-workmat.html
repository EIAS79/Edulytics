7.2 • Burrito prices

Compare10 burritos for$12 with6 for$7.50. Find dollars per burrito and the price for60 burritos, using each special only. Explain why the first special is cheaper by3 dollars overall.

7.3–7.4 • Unit rates across rows

A salsa recipe has2 ounces onions for16 ounces tomatoes, and6 for48. Compute tomatoes per ounce of onions for both and identify the invariant. Create a third equivalent row and explain the method.
