Constant Speed

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

9.1–9.2 • Run 10 meters

Measure and record each runner's actual time for a 10-meter course. Predict how much time 50 meters would take at the same assumed constant speed. Repeat the measurement and discuss whether real movement is perfectly constant.

Speed is a ratio, not just distance

A cargo ship travels150 nautical miles in6hours, or25 nautical miles each hour at constant speed. Explain why a longer distance does not establish faster speed without comparing time.

Independent Edulytics Cool-down

A model train travels120 meters in8 seconds at a constant speed. Find the speed per second, the distance traveled after5 seconds, and time to travel180 meters. Create a labeled double number line.

Teacher rubric: 120÷8=15 meters per second;5 seconds corresponds to75 meters;180 meters corresponds to12 seconds.
