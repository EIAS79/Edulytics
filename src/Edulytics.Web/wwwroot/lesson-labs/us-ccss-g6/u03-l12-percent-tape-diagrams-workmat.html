Independent Edulytics Cool-down

Twenty-four students represent75% of a school club. How many students are in the whole club? Draw a tape with four equal quarters and label the known three quarters.

Teacher rubric: Each of three quarters represents8 students; full four quarters is32. Teacher checks three equal known segments, fourth8, reference100% and explanation.
