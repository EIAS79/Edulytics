Distances on a Coordinate Plane

First-party Edulytics learning activity. Numerical examples are precise; student-created diagrams and explanations receive teacher review.

14.1 • Find distances using signed coordinates

Horizontal segments share y. Vertical segments share x. Distance along a shared axis is the absolute difference of the changed coordinate, even when the endpoints have opposite signs.

14.2–14.3 • Four axis-aligned moves

Starting at P=(-2,-1), plot three-unit moves right(1,-1), left(-5,-1), up(-2,2), down(-2,-4). These are four examples of points three units away; other non-axis-aligned points exist too.

Interpret coordinates in a recipe
