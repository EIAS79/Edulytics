2.1–2.2 • Positive, negative and opposite

Opposites are equal distances from0 on opposite sides. The opposite of-5 is5; the opposite of.875 is-.875; zero is its own opposite.

2.3 • Fold the line at zero

Use a folded paper number line with0 at its fold. Mark -1.5 and+1.5; show they line up. Mark -4/3 and explain why it is not exactly at -1.5.
