## Non-negotiable engineering and academic rules
- Select the next **actually unaccepted** lesson/grade from the current authoritative manifest and ledger; preserve all previous valid first-party Rich V2, Practice, source crosswalk, artifacts, and code.
- For every affected lesson, genuinely compare source teacher/student goals and tasks, Addressing versus Building Towards standards, correct mathematics, representations, licensing, learner-visible content, exact Practice and grading. Classify `READY_REUSE`, proven defect, missing shared capability or real blocker. Neither source-edition age nor missing original publisher artwork by itself proves incorrect content.
- Reuse working implementations. Test affected positive, wrong, malformed and boundary submissions; require authentic student/tenant isolation, persisted outcomes and accessible rendering where relevant. Never invent learner/teacher participation, source-PDF inspection, tests, sign-off or proof.
- Track genuine stage status and evidence bound to the tested code SHA. Never mark `D_VERIFIED`, `academicApproved`, `APPROVED` or `RELEASED` merely because of a document edit, incomplete CI or another grade's historical acceptance. Do not re-certify past lessons without evidence of relevant change.
- Do not require optional independent publisher certification or classroom attendance as an automatically imposed gate on first-party digitally authored material; preserve actual owner publication decisions, academic honesty, copyright/source restrictions and all applicable technical, security, CI and release gates.
- Do not weaken required checks or branch protections. A candidate is not published merely by passing tests; verify merge, exact deployed SHA, Render health, student flows and recorded owner UAT independently as applicable.
- **Our-CS is source-reading-only** unless a separately authorized isolated test clone is used. Never reset its source data. Never change production/student/school/tenant data, paid plans or live infrastructure as a side effect of repository documentation cleanup.

**When a historical plan conflicts with current code, a merged PR, or the live ledger, stop using the old historical *execution cursor* and reconcile the facts. Preserve the historical quality criteria and evidence rather than deleting them.**
