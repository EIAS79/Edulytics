## Original assessment items

1. Factor `١٢س²+١٨س` as `٦س(٢س+٣)`, and explain why `٣س(٤س+٦)` is not the fully factored greatest-common-monomial form. Verify by multiplication.
2. Factor `٣س²+٦س+٢س+٤` by grouping, obtaining `(٣س+٢)(س+٢)`. Draw a 2×2 rectangular area model showing four raw terms.
3. Solve `س²=٣س`: `س(س−٣)=٠`, roots `٠` and `٣`. A response with only `٣` loses the zero root and is incorrect.
4. Contextual projectile model `h(t)=−١٦t²+٢٠t`: physical heights are zero at `t=٠` and `t=١٫٢٥` seconds. Explain why the latter is return time and why negative time cannot represent seconds after launch.
