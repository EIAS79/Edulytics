# Grade10 General G10-U01-L15 — Rubric for roots, multiplicity and complex zeros

**Original source:** `01متقدم_.pdf` Advanced Arabic 2025–2026 Volume1, Unit3 Lesson3-7 `الجذور والأصفار` PDF249–256/printed232–239 and calculator-lab PDF257/printed240. Sole owner-approved General curriculum of 148 lessons.

| Evidence dimension | 2 — secure | 1 — partial | 0 — incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fundamental theorem | Distinguishes degree n total complex roots counting multiplicity from number of distinct real x-intercepts | Correct degree but weak explanation of repetition | Treats x-intercepts as all n roots |
| Repeated roots | Identifies both repeated factors and graph crossing/touching, and sums multiplicities | Correct root values but ignores a repetition | Miscounts total roots or misses a repeated factor |
| Nonreal roots | Uses i²=−1 and solves real-coefficient complex zero pairs correctly | One sign or simplification slip | Declares no complex zeros because graph has no intersection |
| Conjugate theorem | Includes a−bi whenever nonreal a+bi is a zero and constructs a real quadratic factor | Correct conjugate but incomplete factor proof | Omits partner or uses incorrect sign |
| Descartes signs | Checks nonzero coefficients of f(x) and f(−x), counts variations and lists possibilities differing by 2 | Correct variation count but falsely assumes exact root count | Counts zero terms as sign changes or reports impossible count |
| Root solving and factorization | Checks every candidate through substitution/synthetic division, finds remaining factors, and verifies degree-complete solution set | Correct partial factors or roots | Treats quadratic-only formula as complete solution of arbitrary higher degree |
| Graph/technology | Labels real x-intercepts, identifies touch vs cross, presents a valid calculator window and explains missing nonreal roots | Plot adequate but no precision/domain discussion | Treats pixel estimates as complex zeros |
| Contextual model | Restricts time/length/profit roots to valid physical interval and interprets units | Partially correct context | Uses physically meaningless root |

**Original task A:** compare `د(س)=(س−١)²(س+٢)` with its plot: state three roots counting repetition, two distinct real zeros, a touch at س=١ and a crossing at س=−٢, with an explanation. A learner must not state that the theorem guarantees three *different* x-intercepts.

**Original task B:** given real-coefficient polynomial zeros −١ and ٣+٢i, construct the missing conjugate zero ٣−٢i and the degree-three monic polynomial `(س+١)((س−٣)²+٤)=س³−٥س²+٧س+١٣`, justifying the factorization.

**Original task C:** `د(س)=س³−٢س²+٣س−٤` has three positive sign changes and zero negative sign changes. The allowable number of positive real zeros is **3 or 1**, not necessarily 3; show the sign-count process and explain the even reduction rule.

**Contextual/technology work:** source-inspired original profit `د(ز)=ز²−٦ز+٨` on `٠≤ز≤٥` has zeros 2,4; require units/domain and a graphing-calculator check. Real-graph x-intercepts cannot display nonreal complex zeros. Approximate or repeated zeros and full graphical evidence remain **TEACHER_REVIEW_REQUIRED**.
