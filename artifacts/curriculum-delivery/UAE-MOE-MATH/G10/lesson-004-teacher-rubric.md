# Grade 10 General U01L04 — Teacher review rubric for original special-product tasks

**Source:** General `01.pdf` Volume1 (2025–2026) PDF pp41–46 / printed28–33. **Lesson:** «نواتج الضرب الخاصة». **Purpose:** first-party teacher assessment for open algebra-tile figures, area relations, signed reasoning and writing.

| Skill/evidence | 2 points — secure | 1 point — partial | 0 points — incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Squared sum | Computes both squares and double middle product | One arithmetic slip | Omits middle term |
| Squared difference | Negative double middle term, positive last square | Correct pattern with slip | Incorrect sign of squared constant |
| Conjugates | Cancels the equal/opposite middle products and retains difference of squares | Correct result with weak explanation | Treats conjugates as a square |
| Structural decision | Identifies the correct pattern or falls back to all-term distribution | One pattern selected with incomplete justification | Applies a special shortcut to unrelated factors |
| Original area diagrams | Accurate square/rectangular areas, signs, labeled positive dimensions, correct squared units | One region or label missing | Invalid area or dimension |
| Mathematical writing | Clearly explains both cross terms and why they cancel or double | Partial reasoning | Pure memorization without supported reasoning |

**Original check:** `(٢س−٣)² = ٤س²−١٢س+٩`, and `(٣س+٢)(٣س−٢)=٩س²−٤`. At `س=٢` the left and right expressions equal 1 and 32 respectively. The teacher checks all terms and justification rather than using only final answers.

**Original drawing:** partition a square of side `س+٢` into areas `س²`, `٢س`, `٢س`, `٤`. At `س=٣`, its side is 5 cm and area 25 cm². Original learner drawing and explanation require human assessment.

**Digital boundary:** the two reused Grade9 exact families assess a single coefficient of a square or conjugate product. The first-party Grade10 `full_special_product` assesses the complete simplified quadratic expression with the independent multiplication verifier. Stage D tests, RTL UI execution and production routing are **not run/activated**; non-digitizable graphics and proof remain `TEACHER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
