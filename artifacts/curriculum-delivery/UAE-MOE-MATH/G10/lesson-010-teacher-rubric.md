# UAE Grade 10 General — canonical G10-U01-L10 teacher rubric

**Lesson:** «قسمة كثيرات الحدود». **Source:** Advanced Arabic 2025–2026 Volume1, original PDF `01متقدم_.pdf`, pp204–212 / printed187–195. This is a distinct goal merged into the owner-approved **General only** course. All scored activities below are independently authored.

| Assessment element | 2 points — secure | 1 point — partial | 0 points — incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monomial division, including multivariables | Divides every term and subtracts exponents separately for each variable; states nonzero divisor | One exponent/coefficient slip | Treats division as subtraction of coefficients or ignores a variable |
| Long division layout | Orders all powers, inserts zero placeholders, multiplies/subtracts successive leading terms correctly | One intermediate arithmetic omission | Disordered division or drops skipped degree |
| Synthetic division preconditions | Uses r for divisor س−ر with leading coefficient 1 and rejects unmodified nonmonic divisor | Correct algorithm with one sign error | Uses wrong r or treats ٣س+١ as س−٣ |
| Quotient/remainder | Confirms المقسوم = المقسوم عليه × الخارج + الباقي and remainder lower degree | Checks one part but not both | Discards remainder or accepts remainder degree ≥ divisor |
| Geometric area model | Builds original polynomial-area rectangle with correct side lengths, positivity conditions and squared units | One label/unit missing | Claims impossible negative length or inconsistent area |
| CAS/technology lab | Produces reproducible quotient and remainder, compares with manual long/synthetic division and interprets differences | Uses calculator correctly but no independent check | Treats CAS screenshot as a proof without reasoning |
| Error analysis / explanation | Identifies incorrect claims about a zero remainder or missing degree with a counterexample | Correct result but incomplete explanation | Repeats invalid mathematical claim |

**Original golden checks:** `(س²+٣س−٤٠) ÷ (س−٥) = س+٨`, remainder 0; `(٢س³−٣س²+٤س+١) ÷ (س−٢)` gives quotient `٢س²+س+٦), remainder 13. Check `(س−٢)(٢س²+س+٦)+١٣=٢س³−٣س²+٤س+١`.

**Technology and high-degree boundary:** polynomial long division by quadratic, full multivariable or rational-coefficient division, and CAS lab pp194–195 require teacher review or a separately justified exact contract. Do not silently represent those as covered by the bounded monic linear exact family.
