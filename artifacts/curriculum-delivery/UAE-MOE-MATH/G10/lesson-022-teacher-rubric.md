**Original source books:** General `01.pdf` PDF137–142/printed124–129; Advanced 4-5 `01متقدم_.pdf` PDF304–310/printed287–293.

| Dimension | 2 — secure | 1 — partial | 0 — incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Add/subtract | Simplifies first, combines identical radicals only | Correct method with coefficient slip | Adds unlike radicals |
| Multiplication | Distributes all terms, uses √a×√b property with valid domains | One omitted term | Multiplies radicands incorrectly |
| Division/rationalization | States nonzero denominator, multiplies numerator and denominator by correct factor/conjugate | Correct answer but weak justification | Divides by zero or changes fraction value |
| Conjugate proof | Demonstrates cancellation of two opposite terms and correct result | Missing one justification | Wrong sign or missing terms |
| Geometry and context | Labels positive dimensions and correct square units; independently verifies | Minor label/units issue | Unphysical dimension or no verification |
| Mathematical writing | Contrasts like/unlike radicals and real domains with counterexample | Correct but incomplete proof | Treats √(a+b) as √a+√b |
