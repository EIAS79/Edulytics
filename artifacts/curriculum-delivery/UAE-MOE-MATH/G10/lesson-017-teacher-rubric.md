# UAE G10 General — U02L01 «خواص ضرب الأسس»: first-party teacher scoring rubric

**Primary source:** owner-provided General `01.pdf`, 2025–2026 Arabic Volume1 physical PDF98–103/printed85–90. PDF104 is cumulative unit review; next Lesson2-2 starts PDF105. **This is A–B–C original assessment evidence**, not imported publisher artwork.

| Source instructional objective | 2 points — complete | 1 point — partial | 0 — incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monomial classification | Correctly distinguishes a monomial with nonnegative integer exponents from a sum or a variable in denominator | Identifies examples but no reason | Treats any algebraic expression as monomial |
| Same-base product | Adds exponents, independently multiplies coefficients and handles different variables | One exponent/coefficient slip | Multiplies exponents or combines unlike bases |
| Power of a power | Multiplies exponents, including nested parentheses | Correct for one level | Adds inner/outer exponents |
| Power of a product | Raises **all** factors, correctly preserves odd/even sign and each variable's degree | One missed factor | Raises only one variable or drops negative coefficient |
| Geometry and dimensions | Original square/triangle/circle/cylinder or rectangular-prism model has valid dimensions, squared/cubed units and correct product rule | Correct formula, one unit/domain omission | Negative lengths or inconsistent volume/area |
| Modeling | Constructs an original car/energy model, checks magnitude and units (e.g. `E=mc²`) | Computation sound, interpretation weak | Arbitrary numerical result without modeled relation |
| Discovery and graph/writing | Explains repeated factor counts, investigates zero/negative exponents as next-lesson bridge, compares (x,x²,x³) graphs | Partial justification | Claims unchecked negative exponent/graph rules based on numeric coincidence |

**Independent author examples:** `(−٣س²ص)³=−٢٧س⁶ص³`; `(٣سص⁴)²×(−٢ص)³=−٧٢س²ص¹¹`; rectangle dimensions `٥س³`, `٣س²`, `س²` give `١٥س⁷` cm³ for `س>٠`. A circle radius `٢سص²` cm has area `٤πس²ص⁴` cm². Teacher verifies full symbolic work and original drawing, not just final number.

**Digital contract limit:** 3 numeric exact families test same-base exponent sum, exponent-of-power product, and signed coefficient of power-of-product.
