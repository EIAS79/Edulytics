# Grade 10 General — canonical G10-U02-L03 teacher assessment rubric

**Lesson:** «الأسس النسبية», original General `01.pdf` scanned PDF113–119, printed100–106; Advanced `01متقدم_.pdf` Unit4 Lesson4-6 PDF311–316, printed294–299.

| Assessment goal | 2 — secure | 1 — partial | 0 — incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rational powers and nth roots | Explains numerator as power and denominator as root index, verifies an inverse power | Correct answer without convincing relation | Treats fractional exponent as scalar multiplication |
| Negative rational power | Uses reciprocal and excludes a zero base | Correct reciprocal with missing domain | Makes answer negative instead of reciprocal |
| Real-valued domain | Distinguishes odd/even root and states nonzero conditions | Identifies some but not all restrictions | Claims even root of negative has real result |
| Exponential equations | Confirms equal positive nonunit base before equating exponents | Correct arithmetic without base justification | Equates exponents of unrelated bases |
| Graph/context/measurement | Uses root/power to interpret original data and units, checks plausible magnitude | Minor omission in modeling units | Ill-defined model with no contextual reasoning |
| Independent written explanation | Includes each conversion, restriction, and reverse check | States only procedure | Conflates exponent subtraction with rational powers |

**Original reference exercises:** Calculate `٢٧^(٢/٣)=٩`, `١٢٥^(−٢/٣)=١/٢٥`, `(−٨)^(١/٣)=−٢`; show why `(−١٦)^(١/٤)` is not real, and solve `٣^(س+٢)=٣⁶` to obtain `س=٤`.

**Assessment boundary:** The authored bounded exact grader covers positive perfect nth powers, reciprocal fraction of a positive perfect nth power, and same-base integer equation solutions. Variable-base radicals, signed-root parity, compound geometric and growth arguments, graph and open writing stay **TEACHER_REVIEW_REQUIRED** and must not be falsely reported as automatically scored. Stage D tests written but not run.
