**Original golden exercises** for teacher scoring: `س²+٩س+٢٠=(س+٤)(س+٥)`; `س²−٨س+١٢=(س−٢)(س−٦)`; `س²+٣س+١` is not a product of integer monic binomials, although its quadratic equation has two real irrational roots; `س²+٥س=٢٤` has roots `−٨` and `٣` after normalization.

**Original geometry:** rectangle length `س+٤`, width `س+١` and area `٥٤` square meters yields roots 5 and −10; only 5 produces positive dimensions (9×6 = 54). An area diagram is teacher-assessed.
