# Grade 10 General U02L04 — «الترميز العلمي» original assessment rubric

Source General `01.pdf` physical PDF121–127 / printed108–114. The worksheet/test pages 128–129 are a separate assessment boundary and not extra lessons.

| Goal | 2 = secure | 1 = partial | 0 = incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scientific form | Clearly states `1≤|a|<10` and integer exponent; correct sign | Correct but minor decimal omission | Leaves `a` outside standard range |
| Positive and negative exponent conversion | Shifts decimal correctly and verifies ordinary form | One decimal shift mistake | Reverses sign systematically |
| Multiplication and division | Calculates coefficients and exponent sums/differences then normalizes | One signed exponent or carry mistake | Omits normalization or divides wrong |
| Addition and subtraction | Aligns powers of ten before adding/subtracting coefficients | Correct method but one arithmetic slip | Adds exponents instead of coefficients |
| Significant digits and units | Reports precision and units appropriate to question | One labeling omission | Unsupported quantity/units |
| Explanation and error detection | Justifies each adjustment and independently checks the result | Explanation incomplete | Unsupported result or wrong unit |
