# Grade 10 General G10-U04-L07 — Teacher review rubric

**Lesson:** العلاقات والدوال التربيعية وتحويلاتها. **Source:** Advanced `01متقدم_.pdf`, technology lab PDF166–167 (printed149–150), formal lesson PDF168–172 (printed151–155). The rubric grades original student work; no publisher illustrations are copied.

| Source goal | 2 — Correct | 1 — Partially correct | 0 — Incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vertex and axis | Correct رأس (هـ،ك) and axis س=هـ; both graph and algebra agree | One coordinate or label error | Axis/vertex unrelated to formula |
| Horizontal and vertical shifts | Explains why (س−هـ)² shifts right for هـ>٠, left for هـ<٠, and ك controls vertical shift | Right direction but numerical error | Reverses both translations |
| Opening/reflection and width | Separately handles sign(a), |a|>1 narrowing, 0<|a|<1 widening | Only one of three effects described | Calls reflection an upward shift or ignores coefficient |
| Completing the square | Exact equality between أ س²+ب س+ج and vertex form, including negative أ | One algebra slip | Non-equivalent equation |
| Plot and symmetry | Cartesian axes with justified scale, plotted symmetric points, vertex and direction labeled | Unlabeled points or scale | Curve unrecognizable or non-symmetric |
| Application and domain | Maximum/minimum justified within physical domain with units (time, AED, area) | Numeric answer correct but domain not checked | Uses vertex outside permissible domain without checking |

**Original reference tasks:** Convert `س²+٦س−٥=(س+٣)²−١٤` and `−٢س²+٨س−٣=−٢(س−٢)²+٥`. For the first the vertex is (−٣،−١٤); for the second (٢،٥). Plot and compare `٢(س−١)²+٣` (minimum at ١،٣) and `−(س+٢)²+٤` (maximum at −٢،٤). Show symmetric points with correct plot labels.
