# Grade 10 General U01L16 — Teacher rubric for rational zero theorem

**Approved canonical lesson:** «نظرية الصفر النسبي» within «كثيرات الحدود والتحليل» (single General curriculum). **Source:** `01متقدم_.pdf`, Arabic 2025–2026 Advanced Volume1 Unit3 Lesson3-8, physical PDF258–263 / printed241–246. Original Edulytics activity and scoring, not publisher figures.

| Goal | 2 — complete | 1 — partial | 0 — missing/incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Candidate formation | Factors of constant and leading coefficient, both signs, reduced and no duplicated ratios | One omission or duplication | Confuses possible with actual roots |
| Exact testing | Exact substitution or synthetic division validates each reported actual root | Minor arithmetic slip | Accepts nonzero remainder as actual zero |
| Remaining zeros | Continues factorization, includes irrational/complex roots and multiplicity where applicable | Correct rational roots but one remaining root missing | Ends at first rational root without reason |
| Mathematical explanation | Explains why p/q divisibility is necessary but not sufficient | Uses formula without complete rationale | Claims all candidates must be zeros |
| Numerical/graphical model | Plotted axis intersections correspond to actual real zeros; graph is not a proof of rationality | Correct approximate reading but no validation | Treats visual estimate as exact rational zero |
| Three-dimensional geometry | Sets volume equation, finds feasible positive dimensions with squared/cubed units and verifies model | Minor units/constraint omission | Accepts negative lengths or inconsistent volume |
| Technology source activity | Synthetic-division table, reasoned candidate pruning, and graph-window selection documented | Most work shown, weak rejection proof | Black-box calculator answer only |

## Independently authored assessment set

1. With `د(س)=٦س²−٥س+١`, list `±١, ±١/٢, ±١/٣, ±١/٦` as **possible** reduced rational zeros. Only `١/٢` and `١/٣` actually make the polynomial zero. A list omitting fractions loses the leading-coefficient information.
2. With `د(س)=٣س³+٢س²+٥س−٢`, prove by exact substitution that `١/٣` is a root and then derive `(٣س−١)(س²+س+٢)`. The other two roots are `(−١±i√٧)/٢`, not additional rationals.
3. A box has side lengths `س`, `س+١`, `س+٣` cm and volume `٣٠` cm³. Write the cubic, identify `س=٢` as a valid positive candidate and confirm `٢×٣×٥=٣٠` cm³. Explain why purely algebraic negative candidates are not physical lengths.

**Boundaries:** three digital exact families assess a bounded cubic candidate count, divisibility membership and actual-zero decision.
