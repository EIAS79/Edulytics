# G10-U03-L08 — independent teacher rubric: inverses and AX=B

Source Advanced Arabic 2025–2026 Vol1 original lesson1-9, PDF91–97 printed74–80; next unit begins PDF98.

| Goal | Secure 2 | Partial 1 | Incorrect 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity and inverse | Demonstrates both orders equal I when invertible | Valid inverse with only one side checked | Incorrect or absent identity |
| 2×2 formula | D≠0; adjugate signs and exact reduced fractions | One sign slip | Divides original A by D without adjugate |

| Fractional result | Keeps exact fractions and verifies all original equations | One arithmetic slip | Rounds without justification |
| Context | Units, product order and pricing/tickets meaning correct | Partial interpretation | Invalid count or matrix layout |
