# G10-U04-L08 — Original teacher rubric: quadratic rate-of-change lab

**Source:** Applied student mathematics `رياضيات كتاب الطالب تطبيقي 10 8.pdf` Grade10 Unit4 lab, **PDF31 / printed214**. The matching `03.pdf` annual table of contents is provenance only, not a duplicate lesson. All original student-facing examples and graph tables in Edulytics are first-party.

| Learning target | 2 points — meets | 1 point — partial | 0 points — incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Table sampling | Correctly fills t and f(t) values at consistent half-second steps | One arithmetic slip | Omits interval or uses wrong formula |
| Original Cartesian graph | Axes/time/height units, point scales, vertex and intervals of increase/decrease correctly labeled | Correct curve but scale/labels weak | Incorrect graph or unscaled list |
| Average rate | Uses (f(v)−f(u))/(v−u) with sign and units (ft/s for height) | Arithmetic or unit error | Confuses difference in height with rate |
| Change of rate | Explains that quadratic average rate changes on consecutive intervals; sign flips across peak | Identifies sign without explanation | Calls rate constant for curved graph |
| Realistic time domain | Limits time and height appropriately; identifies time on ground | Domain not fully justified | Negative time or below-ground heights accepted |
| Physical interpretation | Connects growing/decreasing height to positive/negative average speed and compares adjacent half steps | Correct values with vague physical meaning | Confuses distance with signed rate |

**Source-aligned original exercise:** A test rocket follows `h(t)=−16t²+144t` (feet, t seconds). At t=0,0.5,1,4,4.5,5,9 the heights are 0,68,128,320,324,320,0 feet. The mean rates on [0,0.5], [0.5,1], [4,4.5], [4.5,5] are **136, 120, 8, −8 ft/s**. Height increases to t=4.5 and decreases afterward within [0,9]. The graph and full table remain teacher-reviewed.

**Additional source-aligned tasks:** `y=x²` from −4 to4 has point values 16,9,4,1,0,1,4,9,16. `h(t)=−16t²+100` has physical ground time t=2.5 seconds and mean rates −8 ft/s on [0,0.5] and −24 ft/s on [0.5,1].
