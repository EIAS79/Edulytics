| Learning target | 2 — secure | 1 — partially demonstrated | 0 — incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drawing the two lines on one Cartesian grid | Both axes scaled consistently, at least two correct points per line, graph lines/labels correct | Minor axis or point-label error with the common solution visible | Missing or incorrectly scaled lines |
| Intersection as simultaneous solution | Correct ordered pair marked and verified in BOTH original equations | Correct graph reading but one substitution omitted | Point only satisfies one equation |
| Unique, none and infinite classifications | Different slopes = one solution; same slopes with different intercepts = no solution; same line = infinite solutions | Correct category but one missing justification | Misclassifies parallel versus coincident |

| Written explanation of slope, intercept and graph shape | Clear reasoning why lines meet, are parallel or coincide | Result stated without full explanation | Memorized answer contradicts the graph |

**Original worked check:** `ص=٢س+١` and `ص=−س+٧` intersect at `(٢،٥)`; for `س=٢` both expressions equal 5. At `س=٠`, the lines meet the ص-axis at 1 and 7 respectively. Learner draws both and labels `(٢،٥)`.

**Original distinction:** `ص=٣س−٢` and `ص=٣س+٤` have no intersection; `ص=٢س+١` and `٢ص=٤س+٢` are the same line and share infinitely many points. These categories are not to be replaced by a numeric question that simply evaluates one line.

**Original applied model:** The costs `١٠+٤س` and `٢+٦س` AED intersect at 4 units with equal cost 26 AED. Learner must state what س represents and exclude nonsensical negative quantity/counts where the context requires nonnegative whole units.

**Stage C boundary:** the exact families assess integer x/y coordinates of a unique system or 0/1/2 classification of solution counts.
