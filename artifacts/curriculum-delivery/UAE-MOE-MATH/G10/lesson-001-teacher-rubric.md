**Application rubric (source 01.pdf printed page 12):** A valid rental-cost model must (1) charge the per-day fixed amount once per day; (2) charge the per-kilometre rate against total distance once, not once each day; (3) keep days and kilometres as distinct variables; (4) compare both models only after coherent substitutions with AED units. Example original Edulytics model: `A(d,k)=18d+0.2k`, `B(d,k)=12d+0.1k`, at `d=3,k=240` yields `A=102`, `B=60`, `A+B=162`, `A−B=42`. Human review for the real-world interpretation.
