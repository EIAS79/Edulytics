# Grade10 U04L03 — teacher rubric for original complex-number arithmetic and complex-plane lab

**Primary source:** `01متقدم_.pdf` Advanced Arabic Grade10 2025–2026 Volume1 original Lesson2-4 PDF139–144/printed122–127, related complex-plane lab PDF146–147/printed129–130. PDF145 and PDF148 are reviews/tests, not lesson pages. Original Lesson2-3 PDF131-137 deals with factoring quadratics and is not copied into this canonical complex lesson.

| Mathematical evidence | Secure 2 | Partial 1 | Incorrect 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imaginary power and negative square root | Uses i²=−1, i⁴=1 and simplifies √−k to i√k appropriately | A single exponent sign slip | Treats i as real and sets i²=+1 |
| Equal/sum/difference of complex numbers | Separates real and imaginary coefficients | One sign slip | Adds unlike real/imag terms |
| Full multiplication/division | Correct distributive complex product and conjugate denominator | Correct approach one arithmetic slip | Omits i² or divides sum termwise |
| Negative discriminant equations | Gives true complex roots and verifies substitution | Gives imaginary but not both roots | Says no solution over all complex numbers |
| Complex-plane graphic | Correct real axis horizontal, imaginary vertical; plots (a,b) and vector addition | One labeled coordinate issue | Swaps axes or ignores signs |
| Modulus and conjugate | √(a²+b²) length and product with conjugate a²+b² | Correct idea with arithmetic slip | Treats modulus as a+b |
| Engineering-context explanation | Interprets complex AC voltage/current phasor with assumptions and units | Correct arithmetic weak context | Treats imaginary component as impossible physically |

**Original golden cases:** `(2+3i)(4−2i)=14+8i`. `(4+2i)/(1−i)=1+3i`. For `z=−5+12i`, point (−5,12), |z|=13. Equation x²+16=0 has roots x=±4i.

**Boundary:** Stage C auto-grades only specific exact numeric coefficients, real component of i powers, and **modulus squared**, not full number input, calculator complex plane, or plotting proof. Human-scored source full tasks remain covered via rubric; Stage D tests WRITTEN_NOT_RUN.
