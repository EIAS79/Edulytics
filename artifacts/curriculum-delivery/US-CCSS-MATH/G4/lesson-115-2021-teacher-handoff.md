**Warm-up (10 min) — Which One Doesn't Belong:** Use legally accessible 2021 four quadrilateral figures or new independently drawn figures. Invite alternative justified classifications using parallel sides and square corners. Teacher checks the actual source visuals.

**Activity 1 (15 min) — Spot Lines and Line Segments:** On the original historical Staten Island neighbourhood map, point out straight line segments that are parallel and pairs that intersect. The 2021 source attributes a Sanborn map as public-domain; no source map graphic has been copied into this repo. Teacher must inspect the figure, its attribution, and students' marked geometry.

**Activity 2 (20 min) — Draw and Design with Lines:** Students DRAW part of a classroom containing **at least three parallel segment pairs** and **intersecting segments forming square corners**. Verify at least three distinct parallel segment PAIRS, their supporting-line directions, and a right-angle marking. Three mutually parallel segments already form three valid pairs, so do not invent a requirement for six distinct segments. Ask students to explain which lines are parallel and which intersect. A mere choice of "parallel" cannot satisfy this task.

**First-party Cool-down (5 min):** Draw two different parallel line segments and a transversal, label two intersections, and justify why the original pair remains parallel. This is independent Edulytics assessment, **not** the gated original Word Fun publisher task.
