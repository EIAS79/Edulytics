# Edulytics original Cool-down — Grade 4 U01 L01

**Origin:** first-party Edulytics independent formative assessment, **NOT copied from** the access-restricted Illustrative Mathematics teacher Cool-down. Original 2021 student PDF task and teacher goals were reviewed; original 2021 protected Cool-down was not reviewed.

**Duration:** 5 minutes after the 10-minute synthesis. **Student question:** "A rectangle is 3 units wide and has an area of 25 square units. Can both side lengths be positive whole numbers? Explain with an equation or a grid sketch. Next give an area less than 30 square units that IS possible for width 3, and justify it."

**Answer key for teacher ONLY (do not expose as student prompt):**
- No: 25 ÷ 3 is not a whole number; 3×8=24 and 3×9=27, so 25 square units cannot be achieved.
- Any positive multiple of 3 below 30 is acceptable (3,6,9,12,15,18,21,24,27), e.g. 18 square units = 3×6.
- Mark mathematical reasoning (fixed width, whole-number side, units) and one valid area; don't require a single example; allow a correct grid or equation.
- An automatic numeric yes/no practice variant exists under `usccss.g4.u01.l01.area_is_multiple`; teacher checks the open reasoning and drawing. Do not claim the numeric variant is a complete evaluation of the free-response task.
