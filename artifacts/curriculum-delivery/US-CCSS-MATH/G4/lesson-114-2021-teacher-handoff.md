**Warm-up (10 min) — Curious Figure:** Display a lawfully accessible original or independently drawn multi-segment figure. Students count visible segments and explain distinct decompositions. No numeric count or original diagram fidelity can be certified from a text excerpt.

**Activity 1 (15 min) — Four Lines:** Draw the source triangle formed by three intersecting straight lines on dot paper, then challenge students to draw a fourth line producing a quadrilateral. A candidate may cut across two sides near one corner to form a four-sided region. The teacher assesses the actual construction, bounded sides and reasoning; do not auto-grade an unexplained "yes."

**Activity 2 (20 min) — To Cross or Not to Cross:** Students draw and label line `h` through at least two source dots, then line `g` through at least two dots so the extended lines intersect. Mark the intersection and discuss pairs that do **not** meet when extended (parallel lines). Check labels, drawn line extent, dot placement and mathematical explanation.

**Independent first-party Edulytics Cool-down (5 min):** Draw a labelled pair of parallel lines and a labelled intersecting pair. Mark and explain the intersection. Teacher reviews the drawing.
