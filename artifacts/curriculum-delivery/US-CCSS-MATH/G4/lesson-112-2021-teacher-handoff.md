# G4 2021 U07:L01 — source-led teacher handoff (first-party, not publisher asset)

Source: original local 2021 preparation and teacher lesson: How Would You Describe These Figures? (4.G.A.1). **Formal definitions of line and segment are not required yet**; the next lesson formalizes these terms.

- Warm-up 5 min: show lawful string-art source (or teacher-made independent marks). Students notice/wonder with partner, then refine words about orientation, crossings and point locations. The string-art source credits LWan98 CC-BY-SA 3.0; do not copy/relicense that third-party photograph automatically.
- Activity 1 25 min: set up four original cards if lawfully accessible (or teacher-created equivalent cards), ruler, divider, blank index cards and chart paper. Two rounds with A describing unseen image and B drawing, then switch roles for two rounds. Compare source and drawing, ask which positions, directions and lengths were ambiguous. Display revised words using MLR2.
- Activity 2 15 min: on an isometric dot field with a straightedge draw exactly FIVE long straight lines, each through AT LEAST TWO dots. Highlight a familiar triangle, quadrilateral, letter or number made by selected shorter line pieces. Compare drawings in groups and describe portions with visible start and stop points.
- Synthesis 10 min: point/line/segment vocabulary arises informally. Do not require infinite-line formal distinctions before Unit 7 Lesson 2.
- Independent Edulytics Cool-down 5 min: one student verbally describes a horizontal and a vertical crossing stroke with two labelled points; partner redraws. Teacher checks orientation, crossings, relative locations and revised clarity.

Digital Practice: exact choice of clearer geometric description (six first-party pair modes, randomized order). **Only the description choice is machine-graded**. The original four card drawings, isometric figures, group oral work, student sketches and protected publisher Cool-down are teacher-reviewed, and no source-media or student-renderer parity is claimed.
