# G4 2021 U07:L02 teacher-led handoff

Original 2021 preparation and teacher lesson locally read on Our-CS, 4.G.A.1 (geometry), 4.NBT.B.4 and 4.NBT.B.5 (mental arithmetic warm-up). Original 2021 saved teacher page SHA-256 093dfabcfb1b4bd7099bb0399dfe5fb6ba58902b12815a88010d50746ab04a39.

**Warm-up, 10 minutes:** Ask students to solve 90−45, 270−45, 270−135, 360−135 mentally. Preserve strategies and exact results 45/225/135/225.

**Card Sort, 20 minutes:** Provide legitimately available publisher blackline cards (or independently authored equivalent cards) to groups of 2–4, sort by point, line, ray and segment. Teacher checks illustrated cards, endpoint arrows, spoken justification and a four-column graphic organizer. Explain that a geometric point marks a location even though the visible dot has size.

**Drawing, 15 minutes:** Provide a geometric/isometric dot field and rulers. Teacher reviews individual triangle, rhombus, trapezoid, hexagon, pentagon and rectangle and an uppercase letter, number and lowercase letter using a mixture of segments/rays. A ray has exactly one endpoint and extension in one direction; do not mark letter artworks as correct just because student selected a numeric response.

**Synthesis, 10 minutes:** discuss line (continues both ways), ray (one endpoint), segment (two endpoints) and points not necessarily printed as dots.

**Independent first-party Cool-down, 5 minutes:** draw ray PQ and segment PQ, label correct endpoints and explain arrow beyond Q. Teacher checks construction. Original publisher protected Cool-down not inspected.

**Auto-grade scope:** Only first-party text-based geometry-card classification and the four source number-talk arithmetic tasks are digitally assessed. The publisher figure cards and student drawings are teacher-led. First-party support models do NOT assert graphical source-image fidelity.
